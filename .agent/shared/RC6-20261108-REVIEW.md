## Measurement limitations and identity

The report records GNSS image/OCI identity, robot unit/kernel, source/incarnation,
and distinct development/robot-checkout/deployed-ROS revisions. F9P/921600 and
Pi model are operator-provided; exact receiver firmware/driver/topology were not
independently verified. Do not extrapolate to another baseline.

`GnssStatus.stamp` is a runtime-field receipt, not a position-only measurement
epoch. Rates use sequence deltas within one source/incarnation and a monotonic
clock. Plot coordinates use UTC instants displayed in Europe/Paris. Never
replace acquisition identity with callback arrival or mix clock domains.

20 s probes can miss the short RTK event. Continuous capture begins after mowing
and uses best-effort DDS, not lossless serial recording. Diagnostic ages are
sampled values. Raw numeric fix 7 is 3D_FIX despite the helper's incomplete
label map. No surveyed positioning error was measured.
The helper's global sequence max-minus-min must be segmented by source and
incarnation if identities change; the single observed incarnation makes this
run's delta valid, not that calculation generally safe.
Tool maintenance remains a separate follow-up at
`scripts/validation/hardware_session.py::FIX_TYPES` (add schema values6/7/8)
and `summarize` (segment sequence progression by source/incarnation). This
candidate documents and handles those interpretation limits without changing
the live collector or silently assigning new UGA status/completion credit.
The rate curve is sequence advance per probe interval, not instantaneous wire
rate: the collector's monotonic timestamp precedes its service requests. A
short-window fluctuation can include request timing/phase and is not itself
evidence of receiver-rate instability.

Docker RAM reporting was unusable (0 B). Direct RSS has only 27 late readings;
do not invent earlier values, add shared RSS as unique RAM, or infer leak freedom.
Median CPU per core: collector ~0.10%, listener ~4.19%, correlation monitor
~36.78%; the latter dominates. Collector CPU excludes transient probe children
and Docker-daemon work. This is not an instrumentation on/off benchmark.
